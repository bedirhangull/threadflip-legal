Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

ThreadFlip (“we”, “us”) operates the ThreadFlip mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the App.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, and authentication identifiers when you sign up or sign in (including Apple or Google Sign-In via our auth provider, Supabase).
  • Scan content: photos you capture or select from your library, item metadata (names, categories, estimated values, sale status), and related scan history stored in your account.
  • Subscription data: purchase and entitlement status processed through Apple App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat (product identifiers, renewal status).
  • Preferences: language, marketplace country, and onboarding choices.
  • Device & usage data: basic technical data needed to operate the App (app version, device type, crash logs if enabled by the platform).

2. How we use information

  • Provide item scanning, resale price estimates, and inventory features.
  • Authenticate you and secure your account.
  • Process subscriptions and enforce plan limits (scan quotas).
  • Improve accuracy, reliability, and user experience.
  • Respond to support requests at support@threadflip.app.

3. AI and third-party processing

To price items, scan images and related text may be sent to third-party services, including:

  • Google Gemini — image understanding and search-query generation.
  • SerpApi / marketplace data providers — comparable listing search.
  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Apple / Google — sign-in and in-app purchases.

We do not sell your personal information. Third parties process data according to their own policies and our agreements with them, solely to provide the App.

4. Camera, photos, and permissions

The App requests camera and photo-library access so you can scan items and queue photos for pricing. We do not access your camera or photos except when you initiate a scan or pick an image.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain account and scan data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the App under Profile → Delete account. This removes your profile, scans, photos, and related data from our systems. App Store subscriptions are managed separately through Apple and may continue until you cancel them in your Apple ID settings. We may retain limited billing records as required by law.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted transport (HTTPS), access controls, and row-level security on user data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International users

Your information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. By using the App, you consent to such transfers subject to applicable law.

9. Your choices

  • Update profile settings in the App.
  • Manage subscriptions through Apple or Google account settings.
  • Delete your account in the App under Profile → Delete account.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the revised version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

ThreadFlip is operated by Bedirhan Gül. Questions about this policy: support@threadflip.app
